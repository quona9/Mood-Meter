About and privacy

Your data stays on this device. Everything you log (moods, notes, tags and settings) is stored only in this browser on this phone or computer. There are no accounts and no servers that receive your entries.

Nothing you log is sent anywhere. The only network traffic is the app checking the site it’s hosted on for a newer version of its own files, and that request carries nothing you’ve logged. There are no analytics, ads, trackers or cookies, the fonts are bundled with the app, and it works offline.

The one thing to know: like any website, the service hosting these files can see that your device downloaded the app (its IP address and the time). It never sees anything you log.

You’re in control. Your data leaves this device only if you export it. Clearing this site’s data in your browser, or using “Delete all my data” above, erases it for good, so export a backup now and then if you want to keep it.
